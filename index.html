In Summer 2016, I designed and wrote an Android application for Local Evacuation Alert Verification (LEAV) and a JavaScript web module for LEAV's integration with HURREVAC-eXtended (HVX) during my research internship with the Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief Systems group at MIT's Lincoln Laboratory on Hanscom AFB.

  • LEAV routes hurricane evacuees to shelters, visualizes evacuation zone status in an interactive map, and facilitates communication between Emergency Managers (EMs) and evacuees; sponsored by the US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Science and Technology Directorate (S&T)

  • HVX enables Emergency Managers to update, visualize, and monitor the status of state evacuation zones in the HVX web interface; sponsored by the US Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)
